### 🔴 Connect 4: The Ultimate Grid Battle 🟡

Welcome to **Connect 4**, a classic, fast-paced strategy game brought straight to your terminal! Test your tactical skills in a high-stakes duel where every drop counts. 

### 🎮 How to Play

The rules are simple, but victory requires foresight: 

* **The Grid:** The battle takes place on a standing board featuring a **7x7 grid**.
* **The Warriors:** Players wield tokens of two different sides. In this digital edition, we swap traditional Red and Yellow for tokens **'1'** and **'2'**.
* **The Mechanics:** Players take alternating turns dropping a single token from the top of any chosen column. Gravity does the rest, sending your piece settling into the lowest available empty slot.
* **The Goal:** Be the first to align **four of your pieces consecutively**—whether *horizontally*, *vertically*, or *diagonally*—to claim ultimate victory.
* **The Tie:** If the board completely fills up and no one has achieved a sequence of four, the match ends in a honorable draw.

### 💻 Tech Stack & Customization

This game is engineered in **C++** with a custom-designed terminal layout. 

🎨 **Make it Yours:** The visual board layout is completely custom-built! Feel free to dive into the source code, tweak the UI elements, or swap out '1' and '2' for custom emojis or symbols to make the design uniquely yours. 

### 🚀 Getting Started

Ready to play? You can compile and run the game directly from your terminal using the following standard command: 

bash

g++ Connect4Game.cpp -o Connect4Game
./Connect4Game

Use code with caution.
